# EP4 trending SFX — sourcing + usage reference

Source: myinstants.com, same approach as EP1 (see that episode's `sfx/SFX_NOTES.md` for the
general sourcing method and the licensing caveat — reused here per Gordon's confirmed
"yes, same as EP1" direction for this episode).

## Catalog — what each one is commonly used for

| File | Sound | Commonly used for | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mind_blown.mp3` | "mind = blown" | A reveal/twist moment — used when a fact just changed how you see something. Trimmed to the first 2.0s (the exclamation itself; source clip runs longer). | Vocal exclamation, ~2.0s (trimmed) |
| `alarm_siren.mp3` | Danger Siren Alarm | Signaling an attack/threat in progress. Trimmed to 1.8s (one siren cycle; source loops longer). | Wailing siren, ~1.8s (trimmed) |
| `robot_beep.mp3` | Robotic Beep | A scanner/detection cue — something automated just measured or logged you. Trimmed to 1.5s. | Synthetic beep-boop, ~1.5s (trimmed) |
| `camera_shutter.mp3` | Camera Shutter | A snapshot/ID-check/"gotcha" moment. | Sharp click, 0.88s |
| `over_9000.mp3` | "It's Over 9000!!" | Escalation/power-level jokes — an arms-race or "it just got way more intense" beat. | Shouted exclamation, 2.6s |
| `correct_ding.mp3` | Correct Answer (game-show ding) | Resolution/"solved" moment — reused from EP1 as a recurring series motif for payoff beats. | Bright upward chime, ~1.3s |

## Mixing notes

- Same caveat as EP1: levels (0.5-0.6 relative to narration) are estimated, not confirmed by ear.
- The three longer source clips (mind_blown, alarm_siren, robot_beep) are trimmed to their
  first 1.5-2.0s with a short fade-out rather than played in full, so they punctuate the beat
  instead of ringing into the next line.
