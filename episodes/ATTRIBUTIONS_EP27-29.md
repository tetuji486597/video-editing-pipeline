## Not obtained

- **Simian Army diagram** (Netflix Tech Blog, 2011): netflixtechblog.com/Medium returns 403 to fetchers and the article images are not in the static HTML; skipped (Chaos Monkey logo obtained instead).
- **Full-size Cameraman render**: the only clean transparent Cameraman renders on the Skibidi/GameToons wikis are 263-938px tall; kept the three best. They are flat-shaded, so a 2x lanczos upscale holds.
- **Large Among Us single crewmate**: the wiki sprite is 150x198; crop the red crewmate out of the 1200x502 key-art sheet instead.
- **Chaos Monkey logo is only 225px** at every official location (netflix.github.io, GitHub docs/, Commons); high-contrast black line art, a 4x lanczos upscale is fine.
- **Brr Brr Patapim, Lirili Larila, Cappuccino Assassino** were not requested for this batch and were not sourced.
- **Sounds**: see `sfx_shared/BRAINROT_SFX_NOTES.md` (myinstants.com licensing is unclear; files intentionally not committed, same as the `tt_*` set).
